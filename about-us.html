About Us | Art Hub

Art Hub is a vibrant community initiative dedicated to celebrating creativity and local talent. Our festival brings together artists, workshops, and interactive exhibits for everyone to enjoy. We aim to inspire, educate, and connect art lovers across Burnley and beyond. Join us to explore, create, and experience the power of art.

Our Mission

To provide a platform for artists of all ages, nurture creativity, and foster community engagement through immersive art experiences.

Meet the Team

Our passionate volunteers, local artists, and event organizers work together to make every festival unique and memorable.
