Community Events

Community events bring local artists and visitors together for small exhibitions and live demos. Each event introduces new artwork and simple techniques anyone can try. People of all ages are welcome, making it a relaxed space to meet creators, learn basic skills, and stay involved in the local art community.

Workshops

Our workshops give you simple, hands-on activities taught by friendly volunteers. You learn basic drawing, painting, and craft skills at your own pace. Sessions stay relaxed and beginner-friendly, making it easy to try new techniques, meet other learners, and build confidence with your art.
